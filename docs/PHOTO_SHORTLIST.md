# COCCINELLE – Category photo shortlist

**Read this first.** I could not view or download these photos from my environment, so I have not checked their quality or composition. Open each link, pick the one you like, and check the licence shown on that photo's page before downloading.

- **Unsplash** and **Pexels** photos are free to use on a business website under their own licences. Credit is optional but appreciated. Re-selling the photos as-is is not allowed.
- "Photo page" = a specific photo that appeared in my search. "Search page" = a results page I have not vetted, so choose from it yourself.
- Prefer real photos of your own shelves, counters and products where you can. They are the most authentic.

## How to add a photo

1. Download the photo and save it as the filename shown below (JPG).
2. Put it in the `images/cat/` folder of the repo (GitHub: *Add file > Upload files*).
3. Ideal size: **1200 x 1500 px portrait** (4:5), under **250 KB**. Free tools: squoosh.app or tinypng.com.
4. Until a file is added, the site shows a clean branded placeholder for that category, so nothing breaks.

Also add one wide image `images/hero.jpg` (**2000 x 1200 px**, under 300 KB) for the homepage banner.

## Product photos

Product cards show a branded placeholder until the product has its own photo. Add one per product in the Admin Dashboard. Best results: your own photo on a plain light background, product centred, square crop.
